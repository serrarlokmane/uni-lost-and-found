## For students

### 1. Create your account
1. Click **Log in → Create an account**.
2. Enter your email, a password (8 characters or more), your full name and your **student ID**.
3. You see "Your account is waiting for approval". An admin checks your student ID, usually the same day.
4. Log in again later: once you're approved, you can use the whole site.

### 2. Find a lost item
1. On the home page, type a word in the search box (for example "keys").
2. Use **Lost / Found** and the category list to narrow the results.
3. Click a post to see all its details and its number (for example **No. 16**).

### 3. Post something you lost or found
1. Click **New post**.
2. Choose **Lost** or **Found**, a category, a short title, a description, the place and the date.
3. Click **Publish post**. Your post is checked by an admin before it's public. You can post up to 5 times a day.

### 4. Claim an item
1. Open the post.
2. On a **found** item, click **This is mine**. On a **lost** item, click **I found it**.
3. Write details only the real owner would know (color, what's inside, a mark…). The admin uses them to decide.
4. You can send only one claim per post.

### 5. My page
- **My posts**: each post with its status: Pending, Published, Rejected (with the reason) or Returned.
- **My claims**: each claim with its status. When a claim is accepted, go to the **Lost & Found office with your student card** and say the post number.
- **Close post**: if you got your item back another way, close your post.

## For admins

Click **Admin** in the header. Each tab shows how many items are waiting.

| Tab | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Accounts** | Check the student ID, then **Approve** or **Reject**. **Ban** an approved account if it is misused (its posts are hidden). |
| **Posts** | Read the post, then **Publish**, or **Reject** with a short reason (the student sees it). |
| **Claims** | Compare the message with the post. **Accept** the right one: the post becomes Returned and the other claims are rejected. |
| **Log** | Every decision, with who made it and when. |

**Rules:** you can't review your own post or claim. Hand items over only at the office, after checking the student card and the post number.
